**English** · Download **`StormReport.exe`** below and run it. Nothing to install. Coming from Fortnite Chest Counter or Chest Addiction Counter? Your history and settings are picked up as they are, and an installed Chest Addiction Counter updates itself to this version.

> **Turn on replays in Fortnite**: *Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays: On*, then **sign in with Epic** on the Account tab.

**What's new in 2.0.0**
- **Storm Report**: Chest Addiction Counter becomes a general Fortnite report, signed in with your Epic account
- **Totals from Epic, details from your replays**: matches, wins, eliminations, K/D and time played come from your Epic account; placement, damage, accuracy, assists, chests and treasures from the replays, side by side per mode
- **Account and Locker tabs**: your locker, V-Bucks, purchases and past seasons (read only), and a button that transfers your whole locker to fortnite.gg
- **Treasure collection fills in by itself** from your Epic locker and your replays: no more ticking by hand
- **Overlay**: the big figure can be chests, eliminations or wins, and a stream window for TikTok LIVE Studio (window capture, dark or chroma-key green)
- **From the original project and its forks**: weekly treasure odds, time zone and clock settings, chests added by hand with a history, chests and treasures per hour, better treasure attribution, clearer error for replays without a key, release checksums

Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* (unsigned exe): *More info → Run anyway*. Check the download with `StormReport.exe.sha256`.
